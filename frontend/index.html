Core Philosophy

PREDICT → DECIDE → RECOMMEND → ACT

StockSathi does not just log numbers. It analyzes sales velocity, identifies future stock gaps, compares suppliers, and generates ready-to-execute purchase actions.

1

PREDICT

Understand future demand before stock runs out based on weekly sales patterns.

Example: “Maggi demand projected to rise to 56 packets this week.”
2

DECIDE

Determine precisely what action is required to avoid losing customer sales.

Example: “Current stock of 20 packets will run out in 2.5 days.”
3

RECOMMEND

Get the optimal reorder quantity, timing, and best Mandi supplier.

Example: “Reorder 45 packets from ABC Distributors (24h delivery).”
4

ACT

Turn the recommendation into an actual purchase order with 1-click or voice.

Example: Generate PO and update ledger upon receipt.
